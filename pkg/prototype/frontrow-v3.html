03 · WHAT HOUSE AV CAN'T DO
Hotel AV runs the screens. We give them something to show.

Your venue's AV team handles projection and sound — we work alongside them, not against them. A volunteer can record the night. Neither can put a live close-up of your honoree, or your donors' raised paddles, on the screens at the exact moment your auctioneer makes the ask. That's a broadcast job, and it's the only kind we do.

The recap film is a sponsor-renewal asset, not a cost — one renewed table sponsor typically covers the whole package. We'll also show your auctioneer what live screens do to a paddle raise.

Recording the night and feeding the screens live during the ask are different jobs. Keep the recording — add the broadcast where the money is raised.
